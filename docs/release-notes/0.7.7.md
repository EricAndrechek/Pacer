Two fixes to `pacer`, so paused work stops before a limit and doesn't stampede back afterwards.

## A go-ahead now expires before the limit

Subagents check `pacer status` between steps. That check accepted a "go" for up to 15 minutes, so near a limit, work in flight kept running until the account was full. A "go" now lasts only as long as the current burn rate leaves room: `status` shows `go (until 22:41Z)`, and once that time passes the next check asks Pacer again. With plenty of room nothing changes. Near a limit, sessions get a fresh answer every minute or two and stop in time.

## Resuming waits for real room

After a pause, `pacer wait` resumed as soon as you switched to another login, even one with only a few points left. Every waiting session then started at once and filled it within minutes. `wait` now resumes on a login switch only when the new login has room for 20 minutes at the rate your sessions were using (`--resume-headroom` to change it). A reset still resumes as before.

**Your data is untouched.** No reset, no re-import.
