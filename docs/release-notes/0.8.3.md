Fewer freezes, updates that install on their own, and a forecast view that shows its progress.

**If Pacer only runs in your menu bar:** quit it and reopen it once so this update installs. After that, updates install by themselves while you're not using Pacer.

## Updates
- A downloaded update now installs at a quiet moment instead of waiting for you to quit Pacer, which a menu-bar app never does. While one is waiting, Check for Updates reads "Relaunch to Update".
- Checking for updates offers the newest version, not an older one that was downloaded earlier.

## Smoother
- The first launch after an update no longer freezes while your data is upgraded. If the dashboard is open, it shows "Updating your data…" until it's ready.
- The dashboard's pace chart no longer stalls Pacer while it redraws.
- Tables on Models, History, Projects and the detail sheets no longer freeze while loading their saved columns.
- Pacer no longer freezes while its forecaster is busy, and backs off when your Mac is short of memory.
- The rebuild after an update is about five times faster.

## Forecasts
- The forecast models view says what it's doing (reading your history, scoring, about how long is left, and what's slowing it), with a **Speed up** button that uses more of your Mac's cores while it's plugged in, for up to an hour. Speed up is hidden in Low Power Mode.
- Scoring starts as soon as Pacer has finished starting up, rather than on a timer.
- On Ultra plans, which have no 5-hour window, Pacer no longer forecasts one.
- The weekly burn rate no longer reads 0 %/h between whole-percent readings, and never goes negative after a reset.

## Multiple accounts and the `pacer` command
- `pacer report` says when your other logins have room left.
- `pacer status` checks again after you switch logins, instead of reusing a verdict made for the old account.
- After a `/login` inside a session, sessions that started earlier are judged against every account they might still be billing.
- Accounts with several tokens get fresher readings: their polls are spread out instead of arriving together.

## Privacy
Freezes are reported more reliably, and a check-in is sent when Pacer updates. No new kinds of data are sent. Pacer never sends prompts, projects, files, accounts or costs.

**Your data is untouched.**
